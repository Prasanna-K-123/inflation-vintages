December 2025 CPI: a reconstructed forecast and its audit

Prepared 17 October 2026. Historical forecast cutoff: 2025-12-12T16:01:00+05:30. This is a retrospective publication-aware reconstruction, not a brief actually issued on that date and not a current forecast.

Page 1 - Information available at the historical cutoff

The target is the next first-provisional All-India Combined headline year-on-year CPI inflation release, old base 2012, for December 2025. The latest release at the cutoff was November 2025, published 12 December 2025 at 16:00 IST, with headline inflation 0.71%. Frozen selected persistence therefore forecasts 0.71%. The fixed AR(1) predicts 3.0234% and the seasonal diagnostic 5.22%; model disagreement is not a calibrated probability interval.

The baseline was selected on January-June 2024 development results, RMSE 0.2948 pp across only six releases. No reliable 80% or 95% prediction range is established by that sample. A decision maker should avoid treating the point estimate as a tight range. Later revisions and new-base backcasts are absent from this historical information set. Page 2 - After-release audit and error decomposition

The first December target, released 2026-01-12T16:00:00+05:30, was 1.33%, leaving persistence error -0.62 pp and AR(1) error +1.6934 pp. For this date, AR(1) error = persistence error (-0.62) + adjustment away from the observed lag (+2.3134). The identity explains forecast mechanics, not inflation causes.

Across all 18 final releases, persistence RMSE is 0.8185 pp versus AR(1) 1.6901. The paired squared-error difference is 2.1865 pp squared, with conditional block-bootstrap 95% interval [0.6137, 3.8166]. This summarizes retrospective average-error uncertainty; it is not an interval for this forecast. The larger final error than development error is evidence against assuming stable forecast precision.

Four unavailable lag-final revisions slightly improve apparent AR(1) MSE by 0.01582 pp squared in aggregate. Four target revisions separately change the estimand; they do not replace the primary provisional result. Do not bridge December old-base 1.33% to January new-base 2.75% as a continuous monthly series. Stop this study at December 2025; evaluate any new-base model separately. Short samples, possible later web edits and no external independent review limit conclusions.
